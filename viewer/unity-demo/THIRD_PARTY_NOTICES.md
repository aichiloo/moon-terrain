# Sources and third-party notices

The macro terrain derives from LROC products LINNECRATER, SHACKRDGE02 and MERSENIUS1. Native metadata is preserved in `sources/*_native_metadata.json`; 2 m / 3.5 m source spacing is distinct from synthetic near-field detail.

Ground materials and terrestrial scanned rock assets are from Poly Haven, recorded as CC0. Moon 01, Moon 03, Moon 04, Flat Macro 01 and Dusted 01 source payloads and URLs are listed in `evidence/texture_package_integrity.json`. These are Earth analogue scans, not site-specific lunar in-situ measurements. Rock size, placement and burial are synthetic.

Some rock shapes reuse Apollo sample scans through Space Robotics Bench. The inherited attribution inventory is `sources/V3_SRB-ATTRIBUTIONS.md`; it also lists assets not used by this demo, and does not mean all listed files are present.
